Privacy & Services.

How the InTrique website handles data and which third-party services it uses.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Overview

The InTrique website is a static marketing and download site. It does not require an account and does not directly ask for personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details.

2. Information we collect

We do not operate our own analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information, such as IP addresses, browser types, requested pages, and timestamps, in server logs for security and operational purposes.

3. Cookies and local storage

The website does not set cookies for advertising or analytics. The site itself does not store personal data in your browser.

4. Third-party services

The website may use or link to third-party services, including:

  • Google Fonts — font files are loaded from Google's servers.
  • YouTube — video embeds may load content from youtube-nocookie.com.
  • GitHub Pages — the website is hosted through GitHub Pages.
  • Discord and YouTube links — external links open on those platforms.

These services may process technical data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

5. Downloads

Downloading the InTrique client does not require registration. The installer may connect to the internet for updates, but it does not send personal information to us as part of the download process.

6. Children's privacy

The website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the official repository.
